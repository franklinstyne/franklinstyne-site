AI Foundations for Workplace Productivity

For teams new to AI. Participants learn how generative AI works, how to write effective prompts and how to check AI output, then apply ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude to their own everyday tasks.

Participants leave with: a personal prompt toolkit and finished outputs from their own work tasks

Tools
ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude
Duration
1-day or 2-day
Delivery
In-house, public or live online
Funding
HRD Corp claimable via a registered training provider
Prerequisites
None. Suitable for complete beginners.
Software access
A free or paid account for at least one AI assistant; access set up before the session
Suitable for
Executives, managers, admin, HR, finance, sales and operations teams

Participants will be able to

  • Write clear, structured prompts that produce reliable results
  • Draft emails, reports and proposals faster
  • Summarise documents, research topics and analyse data with AI
  • Choose the right AI tool for each task
  • Check AI output for accuracy and use AI responsibly at work

Tools
Claude, Copilot agents, n8n
Duration
1-day or 2-day
Delivery
In-house, public or live online
Funding
