Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Are your programmes HRD Corp claimable?

Yes, when delivered through an HRD Corp registered training provider. I am an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer (ID 13169), and I provide the trainer documents you need for the grant application.

Can you deliver under our brand?

Yes. Programmes can be delivered under your organisation’s brand, using your templates and training standards, or co-branded if you prefer.

Can you train large groups?

Yes. For large cohorts and multi-batch rollouts, I work with a network of experienced trainers and facilitators who use the same programme design and materials, so every group receives consistent quality.

Do you deliver outside Malaysia?

Yes. I have delivered in Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and India, in person and live online. HRD Corp funding applies only to eligible Malaysian employers.

How much notice do you need?

Two to three weeks is ideal for a tailored programme. Shorter timelines can often be accommodated for established programmes.

Can the programme be customised for one client?

Yes. Most programmes are adapted to the client's industry, job roles and tools, including Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace environments.
