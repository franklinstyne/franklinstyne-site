How I teach

Learn it. Apply it. Present it.

Learning an AI tool is only the start. The real value comes from knowing where it fits in the working day. Drawing on years in management, I build every session around the tasks, decisions and pain points participants actually face, with a structured prompt engineering framework from the first hour.

Every programme ends with a group project. Teams apply AI to a real problem from their work, then present how they solved it and the time it saved. They leave with a working example, and your organisation sees clear evidence of results, which is why so many return for the next programme.

About Franklin

Technology, business and teaching, in one trainer.

MBA, MCIM, PhD (Candidate). AI has been my passion since the 1980s, when I began my career as a computer programmer and systems analyst building expert systems, an early form of AI. I later moved into business development, leading international market expansion as Head of International Business Development for a global company. Over 40 years, that combination has given me a deep understanding of how technology works, how businesses run and how adults learn.

Today I help corporate and government teams across Malaysia, Brunei, Indonesia and India save time on repetitive work with the right AI tools. I am an ICDL AI and Microsoft 365 Copilot certified productivity coach, a Digital Marketing Institute (UK) certified digital marketing professional, an HRD Corp Accredited Trainer and an adjunct MBA lecturer.
